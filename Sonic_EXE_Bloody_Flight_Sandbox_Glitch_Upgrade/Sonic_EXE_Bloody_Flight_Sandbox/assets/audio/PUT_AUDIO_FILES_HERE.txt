CUSTOM AUDIO ASSETS
-------------------
Add your own audio files here using these filenames:
  background.mp3 - background music (looped by default)
  jump.wav       - jump sound
  ring.wav       - ring collection sound
  emerald.wav    - emerald / flight activation sound
  shield.wav     - shield collection sound
  hit.wav        - player hit / game over sound

The game starts background music after you press START (browser autoplay rules).
Missing files are ignored; built-in synthesized sound effects remain available.
You can change paths and volume in config.js or in editor.html.
